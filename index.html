Our story

A hobby that grew
into a shared language

It started the way most good things do — without a plan.

Two friends, one obsession: yarn, needles, and the quiet satisfaction of watching something take shape in your hands. What began as evenings spent figuring out stitches together slowly became a wardrobe — then a way of dressing, then a way of thinking about clothes altogether.

We still design the way we started: for ourselves, for each other, for the people we love. Every piece begins as an idea, gets frogged and re-knit more times than we'd like to admit, and eventually becomes something we're proud to wear into the world.

— with love, from two friends who knit

Philosophy

Good things take time.
Every stitch is proof of it.

Nothing here is mass-made. A single sweater can take weeks — choosing the yarn, testing the gauge, ripping back a whole sleeve because it wasn't quite right. We show that process on purpose. Knitting is teachable, and we love handing it down: swatch by swatch, stitch by stitch, on Knitting at Home, our YouTube channel.

The exclusive pieces

Some things we knit only once. Worn by us, by friends, by family — these pieces exist in a single copy, or two at most. Think of this as a small, wearable gallery.

no. 01
The Lalo Cardigan

Sunshine, worked in spikelets

Over two months, spikelet by spikelet, this one came together as one of our favourite makes to date. The Asian spikelet stitch looks intricate — it isn't, once you see the rhythm in it — and the result moves and catches light like nothing else in the closet.

no. 02
The Red Plush Sweater

Made for slow mornings

A chenille-soft, cable-ribbed turtleneck knit in a single, deliberate red. This one stayed one-of-a-kind on purpose — some pieces are just for us, and this cardinal-red cocoon is one of them.

One of one · not for sale
no. 03
The English Rib Cardigan

Soft armour for cold days

Worked in a luxuriously puffy, double-sided English rib, this long line cardigan is deceptively simple: knits, purls, and patience. Olive green, endlessly wearable, and warm in a way only hand-knit chenille can be.
